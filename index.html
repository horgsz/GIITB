Get it in the BUCKET

Ground once · Wall once · Then the bucket

Rules
  • The placing player sets the bucket in front of the wall, and the feather further back. Highlighted ground shows where placement is allowed.
  • Throw from the feather. The ball must hit the ground once, the wall once, then go in the bucket.
  • Solo: each make adds a point. Keep setting shots and see how high you can score.
  • Everyone throws from that same spot, in order, looping as many times as it takes, until somebody makes it.
  • Once someone is in, every other player gets one throw to steal the point.
  • The last player to make it takes the point and places the bucket and feather next round.
  • The ball keeps bouncing after a broken sequence, but it can no longer score — the HUD marks it dead.
